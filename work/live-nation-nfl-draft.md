*An advertising campaign in support of Live Nation Concerts*

As a Michigan native and Live Nation marketing coordinator, I had the incredible opportunity to contribute advertising work that was featured at the 2024 NFL Draft in Detroit.

The Draft brought over 775,000 attendees to downtown Detroit, and the ads I created for upcoming Michigan shows with Live Nation were displayed across social platforms and on downtown Detroit boards — reaching this massive audience.

> "I feel proud to be one of 775,000+ attendees — and that an audience of this size saw ads I created for upcoming Michigan shows with Live Nation!"

## Campaign Work

Designed promotional materials for concerts and events across Michigan venues, including graphics displayed on Orange Barrel Media screens during the NFL Draft and surrounding events in Detroit.

Featured campaigns included promotions for artists like Green Day at Comerica Park, 21 Savage at Pine Knob Music Theatre, and upcoming shows at The Fillmore and Little Caesars Arena.
