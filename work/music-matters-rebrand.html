MUSIC MATTERS 2022 REBRAND

A complete visual identity refresh for a University of Michigan club

Initial Assessment

The leadership team evaluated the existing logo, identifying strengths like simplicity and readability. However, the blue and yellow color scheme was too closely tied to the university, and the design elements — a pencil, educational font, and wired headphones outline — didn't adequately emphasize music.

Research Phase

Club members were surveyed to identify adjectives that best represent MUSIC Matters. This feedback informed the design direction and helped ensure the rebrand reflected the organization's true identity.

Design Development

The team explored multiple color palettes and logo concepts, incorporating music-focused motifs including guitars, disco balls, CDs, cassettes, headphones, and vinyl records.

Final Design

The team selected a vinyl record-inspired logo concept, combining the preferred main logo with a streamlined shorthand record symbol. The result is a bold, music-forward identity that stands on its own while representing the organization's mission.
