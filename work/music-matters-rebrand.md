*A complete visual identity refresh for a University of Michigan club*

In 2022, MUSIC Matters Leadership felt it was time for a change. We wanted the logo and branding to have a more clear structure, with a defined color palette, timeless design, and a clean logo that conveyed the energy of the organization. Myself, along with three other members of the Leadership team, spearheaded this initiative to revamp MUSIC Matters branding.

## First Steps

To start, we first evaluated the current logo — what we liked about it, what we didn't, and where we wanted to go from there.

**What We Liked:** Easy to look at. Our logo was refined, simple, and easy to read.

**What We Didn't Like:** The blue and yellow color scheme was too closely tied to the university, and the design elements — a pencil, educational font, and wired headphones outline — didn't adequately emphasize music.

We surveyed club members asking what adjectives came to mind for "MUSIC Matters." This feedback informed the design direction.

## What Next?

We pulled inspiration and created color schemes, exploring options that would give MUSIC Matters its own distinct identity while still feeling energetic and music-forward.

## Design Development

We created 5 initial logo designs exploring guitars, disco balls, CDs, cassettes, headphones, and vinyl records.

We loved option three for the main logo, but didn't love the shorthand option and had a hard time walking away from option one's round shorthand record logo.

**TL;DR:** We combined them.

## Putting It Together

After deciding on these main factors, we were so excited to put our new marketing into place, representing MUSIC Matters in a much more authentic and comprehensive way.
